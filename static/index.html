Streaming
Adds a "Sonar Stream" output with its own volume per channel (📡), without EQ, ChatMix or your mutes. In OBS, capture it with an Audio Output Capture. Switching restarts the channels' audio for a moment.
Updates
Pulls and rebuilds Sonar when switched on, 2 minutes after login and then daily (install/update.sh). An update restarts the audio for a moment.
